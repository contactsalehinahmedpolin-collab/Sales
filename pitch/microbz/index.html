Fix one
1

Make the three-month course the easy choice

Today a new customer buys one bottle, gets a dispatch email, and then hears nothing until the bottle runs out. Most mean to reorder. Many don't.

  • Put the three-month course first on every Bio-Live page: a bottle every four weeks, 15% off, free delivery, pause any time. One bottle stays one tap away.
  • On day 21, when the bottle is low, send a short email and text: "About a week left." One tap to continue.
  • On day 60, a letter from you about the farm and the batch.

Fix two
2

Bring your reviews home

Your customers have already done the selling: 282 reviews, lines like "the whole family loves it" and "so easy to take". But they sit on an inactive Feefo account, and your product pages show none of them. Healf shows 4.9 stars beside Bio-Live Revive. Your own shop should too.

  • Import the Feefo history into a Shopify review app, so every product shows its real count.
  • Ask for a review automatically on day 14, and invite to Trustpilot.
  • Use customers' own words in emails and ads, kept within UK ASA rules: their experience, never a cure.
Fix three
3

Take cost out of every bottle you ship

You dropped free delivery because courier prices rose. Part of the answer is what goes in the box.

  • I'll get a written quote from our factories in Guangdong for the same spec: 100% recycled PET 475ml bottle, recyclable cap, wash-off label, and a 3-bottle box sized for Royal Mail's lowest weight band.
  • Landed in Calne, freight and duty included, next to what you pay now.
  • If the saving pays for free delivery on the course, fix one gets stronger.

An honest note: if it doesn't beat your UK supplier, or the recycled content can't be certified to your standard, keep your supplier. You lose nothing by knowing the number.
