# PART 1 | Follow-ups for the 20 already sent

This is the higher-yield half. Touch 1 is done. Do not let it sit.

Schedule: T+3 days, T+7 days, T+14 days. Same thread, same subject line,
reply to your own sent message so it threads. Never start a new subject.

Rule: if someone replies at any point, stop the sequence for that person.

---

## TOUCH 2 (T+3 days) | the one-line bump

Send this as a reply on your own original. Two sentences maximum. The job of
touch 2 is not to persuade, it is to get the thread back to the top of the inbox.

> Bringing this up in case it got buried.
> Still happy to send the three angles over with no call needed if that is easier.
>
> Salehin

That is the whole email. Do not re-pitch. Do not add a paragraph. The offer to
send value without a meeting is what makes it work, because it removes the
thing they are avoiding.

---

## TOUCH 3 (T+7 days) | give the thing away

This is the one that converts. Stop asking and start delivering. Pick the single
sharpest observation you have about their business and hand it over.

Template:

> [FIRST NAME], no call needed. Here is the one I would test first for [COMPANY]:
>
> [ONE SPECIFIC ANGLE, two sentences, built from the evidence column in the CSV]
>
> If it is useful I have two more. If not, no hard feelings and I will stop here.
>
> Salehin

Filled examples for the five sharpest accounts:

**Wild One | Veronica**
> The angle I would test first is the four-person team itself. A brand that
> serves 135,000 visitors a month with four people is a story your customers
> would actually care about, and right now nothing on the site says it.

**fable | Jeremy**
> First test would be splitting the hero video by use case rather than by
> product. Forty-three thousand visitors is enough traffic that the losing
> variable is almost certainly which problem you lead with, not the offer.

**MysteryVibe | Kelly**
> First angle would be leading with the FSA and HSA eligibility rather than
> the clinical papers. Eligibility is a fact the platform cannot flag, and it
> reframes the purchase from indulgence to healthcare in four words.

**Signal Relief | Jon**
> First test would be filming the skeptic rather than the believer. In
> drug-free pain the highest-converting testimonial is usually the person who
> says out loud that they did not think it would work.

**Tuft & Paw | Jackson**
> First thing I would test is a landing page split by cat problem rather than
> by product category. At 171,000 visitors the leak is almost certainly that
> people arrive with a problem and land in a catalogue.

---

## TOUCH 4 (T+14 days) | the close-the-loop

Short, no pressure, gives them an easy exit. This gets a surprising number of
replies because it releases the obligation.

> [FIRST NAME], closing the loop so I stop cluttering your inbox.
>
> If creative volume is not the constraint at [COMPANY] right now, that is a
> good sign and I will leave it there. If it becomes one later, the door is open.
>
> Salehin
